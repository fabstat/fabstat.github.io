## About Me

{{< figure class="avatar"  src="/fabiana.jpg" alt="Picture of Fabiana" >}}

I am currently working as an assistant professor at [Tohoku University](https://www.tohoku.ac.jp/en/). My research focuses on applied mathematics and statistics, specifically Topological Data Analysis and Machine Learning. As part of the [Mathematical Science Center for Co-Creative Society](https://www.mccs.tohoku.ac.jp/index-e.html) and [AIMR](https://www.wpi-aimr.tohoku.ac.jp/en/), I participate in engaging research from several scientific fields such as material science, medical science and operations research. I am also the program coordinator for [G-RIPS Sendai](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/student-research-programs/graduate-level-research-in-industrial-projects-for-students-g-rips-sendai-2024/?tab=overview), a graduate internship program where students from the US and Japan work together to solve problems from industry.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistical Science from [Washington State University](http://math.wsu.edu) in June, 2024. While a graduate student, I worked as an intern at [PNNL](https://www.pnnl.gov)'s Data Science group. Prior to that I was a mathematics and computer science lecturer at the  [University of Washington Bothell](https://www.uwb.edu), shortly after receiving my M.S. in Mathematics (Optimization focus) from  [UW Seattle](https://www.washington.edu).

Note: If you are interested in my research, I am happy to discuss it and collaborate, however I am unable to advise or fund students at this time.
